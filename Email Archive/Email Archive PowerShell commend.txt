Email Archive PowerShell commend

first enable the MRM policy in Microsoft Preview, afterwards follow the commend

1.	Get-Module ExchangeOnlineManagement -ListAvailable
2.	ExchangeOnlineManagement 3.9.2
3.	Uninstall-Module ExchangeOnlineManagement -AllVersions -Force
4.	Install-Module ExchangeOnlineManagement -Scope AllUsers -Force
5.	Get-Module ExchangeOnlineManagement -ListAvailable
6.	Connect-ExchangeOnline

7.	Enable-Mailbox abc@test.com -AutoExpandingArchive 

	*Check the status should showing true
8.	Get-Mailbox abc@test.com | FL AutoExpandingArchiveEnabled 
9.	Enable-Mailbox vipin.sachdeva@acobloom.com -AutoExpandingArchive
10.	Start-ManagedFolderAssistant -Identity vipin.sachdeva@acobloom.com
